# Category-relevant part comparisons

2026-10-13. Final requested checkpoint in the axle, wheel purchase, geared AWD and comparison batch. Technical delivery is separate from user visual acceptance; no later native load/buy-missing-parts control is advanced.

The wheel exclusion was an example, not the whole rule. Every one of the 49 mechanical categories has an explicit potential-effect contract. Shop, fitted-part and inventory comparisons show only relevant build effects; the cart uses the union of hardware actually changed in its copied car. Storage-only purchases do not add car-stat comparisons. A changed value alone cannot pull an unrelated specification into the list. Full-car, dealership and tuning inspections retain the complete catalog.

Selected hardware, retained condition, exact repeated members and applicable installed settings have an expanded **Part changes** block above **Build effects**. This retains shared bars, units, info help, Differences only, collapse state, dyno/no-wear references and explicit cart/apply boundaries. The same filter synchronizes the own-part and general sections. Brakes expose stopping capacity, cooling exposes heat transfer/rejection, cams expose cam geometry, oil pans expose capacity/pickup, gearboxes expose ratios/handover, and rims/tires expose their own corner geometry/mass/contact facts. Unrelated engine, wheel, cooling and transmission properties stay absent. Rim changes do not claim to change a compound's intrinsic lifespan. Mixed rim/tire quality and masses come from the actual members rather than obsolete parent pack metadata. Signed component ratings and current/proposed bar bounds include both builds.

**Safe operating RPM** is the lowest comparable fitted piston/rod/crank and valvetrain constraint, with actual weak-spring condition/fault severity and installed cam demand. The limiting part is named. **Internal torque headroom** is the minimum comparable internal torque strength at the engine port, using the same retained condition factor as running stress. These are distinct from the electronic limiter target and downstream clutch/gearbox/axle ratings: ratios and torque paths make arbitrary cross-shaft minimums invalid. An electronic cut still cannot prevent a mechanical overrev. Missing core hardware or failed speed-rated parts cannot report a healthy speed ceiling. The minimum-limit matrix also exposed a float-versus-double boundary rejection at the existing 1.01f protection clamp; validation now uses the exact float boundaries without changing calibration.
