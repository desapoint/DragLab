# Ratio-matched locked AWD

2026-10-13. Requested follow-up checkpoint, separate from part-comparison presentation.

A standard locked center is 1:1 in axle angular speed. Equal rolling sizes do not force straight-line tire slip; unequal sizes cannot both roll freely at that constraint. Which axle scrubs depends on grip/load. The actual contact solver dissipates sliding work, and existing tire heat/wear consumes it. Wear difficulty still controls deterioration; matching sizes does not prevent ordinary power-induced wheelspin. Steering/turning wind-up is outside this straight-line model.

AWD platforms now offer a Vector Motorsport ratio-matched locked transfer case with interchangeable speed gearsets. It costs **2,500 more than the highest compatible ordinary transfer case**, requires REP 8, adds two kilograms and unlocks the native Drivetrain speed-ratio control. Ordinary hardware cannot acquire this ability through a saved tune. The ratio is **front wheel angular speed / rear wheel angular speed**, distinct from torque allocation. Match **rear outer radius / front outer radius**; e.g. 0.24 m front and 0.36 m rear need 1.500:1. The hardware fixes Locked center mode, and ratios span 0.500–2.000 with 0.010 normal and 0.001 fine increments. Apply/Revert and middle-click reset remain shared controls.

The mass-weighted solver constrains the geared axle means, transforms front output torque, reflected inertia and input speed consistently, and retains post-gear shaft RPM/torque/power telemetry. Driven rolling radius and mismatch warnings use the actual ratio; front drive component stress uses the converted torque. Old snapshots/settings inherit 1:1. A mismatched custom ratio still scrubs and wears the tires; this is not a blanket grip or damage exemption.
