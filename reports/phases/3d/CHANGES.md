# 3D — paid operations, salvage and recovery (2026-10-09)

Normal play now charges for accepted fuel and fluid quantities, oil/filter service, coolant flushing, applicable fault repairs, wet-clutch oil, individual replacements, rebuilds, new parts and dealer packages. Captured payment quotes freeze requests/prices and revalidate authoritative revision, contents, catalogue/policy and expiry inside the candidate/save transaction. Existing setup-library missing purchases retain their captured-price atomic Apply. Isolated physical/audio benches remain free; no second driving solver or difficulty change was introduced.

Buyer valuations now support explicitly confirmed loose-pack/individual-element sales, attached/stored complete/partial engine sales, complete/incomplete car sales and chassis salvage. Chassis salvage automatically retains the exact engine tree with individual histories/faults and oil/coolant. Selected excluded packs and children become inventory; other stored/loose assets and saved blueprints remain owned. Engine sale can retain its chassis and fuel. Selection moves to another real owned car or becomes empty. Cancel, stale/expired requests, reservations, active runs, insufficient funds, overflow and failed persistence cannot partially dispose or charge assets. Operation IDs, revisions and actual ownership removal prevent duplicate settlement.

Player schema 5 accepts a genuinely empty garage and migrates schema 4 with `.legacy-v4` retained. Startup and preview/selection interfaces no longer assume a car exists. Driving is unavailable until an actual car is acquired; retained assets, preferences, settings and recovery are accessible. The latest 256 committed operating receipts record matching cash before/after, accepted resource amounts and disposed/retained identities. Backup recovery restores a complete previous transaction state rather than mixing old assets and new cash.

Recovery work is one persisted real-time job, available below an authored cash ceiling, with captured deadline/reward and one-time claim. Default policy: 30 seconds, up to $500, eligible below $10,000. Claims create neither parts nor repairs. This provides a bounded route from zero cash and no car to fuel, service or an unrestricted playable replacement while retaining stored assets. Existing idle claims remain available; expanded business/reservation lifecycle remains 3F and full-loop balance remains 3G.

The Resources `OperatingPolicy` ScriptableObject exposes fuel/oil/coolant/clutch-oil rates, repair/service labour, dealer spread, quote lifetime and recovery values. Prepare creates missing data and preserves edits. Default fuel is $2/L, oil and clutch oil $6/L, coolant $3/L; basic applicable repair is $15 labour plus 8% of component catalogue price, rounded up. Top-ups retain aging/faults; services do not restore unrelated structural wear. Oil/filter changes retain the dirty original. Rebuild payments now include oil supplied at collection and the saved job shows the full actual paid amount without a second collection charge.

Earlier dealer MSRP was sometimes below immediate OEM resale value. A provisional package floor uses the maximum stock whole-car/engine-plus-chassis buyer value, a dealer spread and supplied fluids, while retaining higher authored MSRP. All eight default stock packages cannot profit from immediate assembly disposal. This safeguard does not claim final market calibration, intentional rebuild/part-out profitability or robust balance under arbitrary author edits; those remain 3G.
