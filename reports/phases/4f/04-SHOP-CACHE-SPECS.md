# Shop stability, cached inspections and relevant specs (2026-10-13)

User requested a stable, faster shop, representative component specs, individual tires and reuse of calculated part/car data. This is one requested shop refinement; saved-setup browsing remains the next separate control.

## Findings and changes

The old browser created controls for the entire section, calculated full-car candidate inspections continuously, and reapplied filters/order as each result arrived. Its initial Calculating compatibility state excluded items until evaluation completed, so cards appeared/reflowed during ordinary entry. Main-thread work and repeated whole-root button queries amplified the delay. Browsing was already read-only; saving was not the cause identified here.

Fitment is now classified before display without a dyno. Ordinary name/price/search/type browsing has no background candidate inspections. Only matching cards are created, and fixed-height two-column cards reserve their title/spec/condition/price areas. The four component-specific facts reuse the same definition-driven summary on details; full changes/dyno/warnings are calculated on demand there. Metric filters/sorts request only applicable unfinished comparisons, publish their filtered order together and keep a concise calculation state until ready, rather than moving cards result by result. Repeated cached results hydrate immediately. A unique requested comparison still runs the authoritative full resolver; nonlinear effects are not approximated by adding cached HP deltas.

One bounded session cache shares current and planned static inspections, copied planned profiles and candidate comparisons across Shop/Installed/Inventory/Cart. It checks selected identity, profile revision/reference, applied tune, fuel/liquids, actual fitted identities/condition and definition data. Candidate keys include incoming retained part/unit state and selected position. Planned input fingerprints include the captured profile, cart and offer definitions. Snapshot outputs are read-only; price/availability/quote/checkout validation is still live. Definition-based summaries have a separate bounded cache. No player save, ownership normalization/healing, simulation model or part balance is changed.

Tires prioritize grip factor, reference lifespan in km, per-tire slip-work budget and best-grip temperature. These are the actual fictional simulation parameters, not guaranteed lifespan or real manufacturer ratings; load, pressure, heat, slip, condition and wear difficulty alter life/grip. Rims prioritize mass/mass factor rather than claiming tire grip. Other categories emphasize their actual cooling, clutch, gearbox, cam/spring, piston, airflow, service or fluid capabilities. Applicable units and concise explanations are retained. Complete tire prices say set of 4; retained individual tires say per tire. Computed single-unit condition differences remain available after selecting a position.

The purchase dialog labels Tire set (4) / Single tire. Individual buying is always reachable. Selecting it for another specification changes the draft destination to Store and synchronizes both joined controls; one loose tire is staged at its real unit price. Same-specification installation retains explicit FL/FR/RL/RR targets, incoming wear and removed originals. Different compound specifications on individual corners remain outside the current single-definition tire-pack simulation; changing compound for installation still requires a matched complete set. No unsupported mixed-compound behavior is silently enabled.
