# 4D.4 — Quick maintenance on car details (2026-10-10)

User requested main-page liquid refill/condition, quick repairs and exact same-spec purchases for worn/failed installed parts, then clarified oil changes and other maintenance actions. This checkpoint implements that section only; it does not advance browsers, dealership, Build/Tuning shortcuts, history or full inspection/dyno.

- Show fuel, engine-oil and coolant quantities/installed capacities, with oil quality and a general failed/needs-service summary. Installed wet clutches also show their dedicated oil bath/quality. These are the currently simulated liquids; extra brake/transmission/hydraulic reservoirs are not invented. Missing engines retain their stored fluids and expose no chassis oil/coolant controls.
- Shared refill form defaults to free capacity, supports slider/numeric quantity, displays cost/cash and blocks zero, over-capacity, invalid or unaffordable publication. Drafts/cancellation do not alter resources or save. Confirm uses existing PaidEconomy/MechanicsTransactions/ProfileStore; no free race resources or duplicate simulation are introduced.
- Oil change includes fresh oil, the authored replacement filter and labour; coolant flush renews coolant; wet-clutch service renews its bath. Top-up retains oil aging, leaks and wear. Excess-oil drain uses the existing free drain behavior. No action automatically repairs unrelated components.
- List failed/heavily worn/service-fault installed parts using the existing 50% health and fault thresholds, including individual engine/tire faults. Expose Clean, Adjust, Reseal and Recondition only where ServiceOperations permits. Reconditioning remains partial/limited; catastrophic failures need replacement.
- Buy same purchases/installs the exact definition, with complete matched piston/other multiple/tire sets as applicable. The removed item/set retains identity and condition in inventory; saved tuning is preserved. This quick path does not implement individual-element selection, donor/spare browsing or a new cart.
- Reuse the existing paid engine rebuild with real-time/offline duration, reservation, live countdown, gated collection and explicit collection confirmation. This retains existing core/valvetrain scope, partial reconditioning/new matched sets and oil/filter renewal; other systems remain separate.
- All operations target the inspected actual car, including non-current cars, without changing selection. Capture revision/full profile snapshot; stale/missing targets, changed pricing/assets, invalid damage service, reservations and persistence failure reject with an inline error. Failed saves keep drafts for retry and retain cash/resources/condition.
- Reuse the native panel/dialog, buttons, numeric fields and sliders; add read-only level meters. The details preview uses less empty vertical space to accommodate maintenance, with inner scrolling for many faults. Existing header, names/pencil, Back, F8, Settings, no-focus/no-Tab and mouse-down/hover behavior remain.
- Pricing is previewed without save or quote hashing on slider movement. A real immutable paid quote is created only at confirmation, after matching the displayed snapshot/cost. Released detail controls remove their shared animation surfaces; rebuilding the page does not accumulate detached button tickers.
