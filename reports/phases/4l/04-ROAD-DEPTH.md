# 4L.4 — road depth, speed feedback and instruments (2026-10-14)

The requested road angle is **depth perspective, not an incline**. Travel and cars remain horizontal. A shared near/far projection foreshortens the asphalt, markers, opponent and cross-track finish line. The distant car is smaller; nearby detail moves faster. Visual travel is 0.60 world units per simulated metre (previously 0.18), consistently used for separation and finish placement. Race distance, physics, elapsed time and outcomes are unchanged. Cached, deterministic fine grit/aggregate and rubber-darkened lanes replace the stretched placeholder asphalt; stronger road-speed-driven near-ground cues are bounded and freeze with physical travel. Texture creation is cached, mesh buffers/pools reused, and no gameplay RNG, background simulation or per-frame texture generation is introduced.

The start tree now has paired shaded amber/green/red lenses, soft light halos and separate pre-stage/stage lamps; countdown and false-start rules remain. The steering rim draws in front of both gauges, is lower and has a wider black band. Smaller unpadded number boxes and a size-aware inset clear the tick marks. The native single shift lamp appears only with fitted shift-light hardware, follows its applied threshold and remains dark below it, in staging/neutral, during shifts and at top gear; CVT hides it. No recommended shift tick is added to the RPM face. Legacy target text/tick now require the part too. The redline still represents safe hardware RPM independently. Multiple-lamp hardware remains deferred: programmability does not imply extra lamps.
