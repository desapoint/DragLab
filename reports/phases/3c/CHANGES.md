# 3C — valuation and captured quote checkpoint (2026-10-09)

Read-only valuations now distinguish actual owned loose component packs, singular engine elements, attached/stored complete/partial engines, rolling chassis and complete/incomplete cars. Prices explain catalogue basis, authored quality demand, shared pack/individual condition, deposits/leak/adjustment faults, bounded use/abuse/service history, required completeness and authored buyer/model context. Servicing counters alone cannot create resale bonuses. A complete engine can quote above its comparable loose-part subtotal to a builder and below it to a wholesaler. Whole-car context is separate from chassis and engine context; no whole-car purchase price is added on top of its components.

Kept parts and all their individual children are excluded. Engine and chassis identities partition the actual car without overlap. A chassis quote keeps the engine out even while attached; stored/loose assets and blueprints never inflate a car quote. Missing components contribute $0; an empty engine identity has no free core value. Required missing hardware reduces completeness, while stripped interior and other genuinely optional omissions do not create an incomplete-build discount. Fault risk remains visible independently of combustion/running availability.

Quote schema 1 receipts have immutable totals, explanations and copied included/retained IDs, policy ID/version, profile revision, issue/expiry times and a captured-state SHA-256 fingerprint. Default lifetime is five minutes. Expired/backwards-time, stale revision, changed ownership/condition/tune/resources, relevant catalogue definitions or policy edits invalidate them, including changes without a version bump. The snapshot conservatively includes the whole authoritative profile; unrelated garage/preference changes can also require refresh. UI drafts are excluded. Rebuild reservations and unsettled runs permit inspection but block disposal validation. Exact unchanged save/reload validates against the receipt.

Garage, Collection, Parts inventory and stored-engine rows open the exact asset's valuation. Buyer buttons, car/engine/chassis selection, explicit Refresh/Validate, all-adjustment details and individual keep/exclude controls work through actual Windows mouse events. The existing IMGUI prototype remains; explanatory text wraps. Stale offers stay visible until explicitly refreshed. New controls do not sell, charge/refund, refill, heal or detach assets. Existing provisional loose sales and saved-setup purchases are preserved; quote-backed paid operations remain 3D.

The new Resources `ValuationPolicy` ScriptableObject exposes buyer contexts, model chassis/demand values, quality demand, condition/history factors, lifetime and policy identity/version in Unity's Inspector. Prepare creates it only when missing and preserves author edits. The verification menu/CLI exports reproducible CSV scenarios and readable assertions. Quote fingerprint work scales with owned definitions rather than every unowned shop offer, avoiding needless catalogue-wide serialization in the interface.

Final targeted gate: **2,405 checks**, covering all eight cars and every fitted category with wear, failure, individual faults, retained/missing/optional components, engine/chassis partitioning, stored engines, empty identities, history, mixed forged/OEM hardware, authored model demand, service faults, singular-element sale, immutable caller requests, policy/catalogue edits, invalid ownership, expiry and reservations. **1,057 scenario rows** are exported in `valuation-matrix.csv`. `verification.txt` contains every assertion. Scene initialization/eight-car wiring, reward/save/backup integration and Windows build pass. Native accepted fixtures in `reports/player-fixtures`: `phase-3c-valuation-first` (14 semantic checks), `phase-3c-assemblies-setups-regression` (13), `phase-3c-parts-regression` (41). All three pass with **zero runtime/GUI/DSP errors**; valuation summary, keep-engine and adjustment screenshots were inspected. This is 68 native semantic checks across three workflows, not a fresh rerun of the earlier full foundation suite.

Player save schema remains 4, mechanics schema 1 and shared physics model v15. No new solver or migration was introduced. The 2L 8,877-check foundation report is retained as an explicitly prior baseline. The 3A/3B archives remain separate and unchanged. Current source and the complete Windows folder are captured separately for 3C; the player requires its data directory and Unity runtime companions.

Limits: prices are provisional and fictional, not real-market quotes. Actual quote-backed cash/ownership mutations, retained-engine salvage, paid refuelling and basic recovery are **3D**; full-loop pricing/recovery balance is **3G**. Quotes are not persisted transaction authorizations yet. New cars retain layered placeholder artwork. Android/device and human driving/listening acceptance remain separate.
