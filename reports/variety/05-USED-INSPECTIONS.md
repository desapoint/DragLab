# Paid used-car inspection and retained risks — 2026-10-11

This implements the separately requested dealership risk change. The four variety checkpoints remain delivered in the preceding commit; no later UI page is advanced. User acceptance of presentation and balance is separate from technical checks.

Normal saved used listings now include seeded service history, possible unrepaired engine/brake/clutch/shaft/pump failures, compression faults or cooling leaks. Fuel, oil and coolant quantities vary independently. Runs/drives observations use an isolated copy of the authoritative simulation; braking and propulsion failures retain their independent consequences. Reloading or browsing cannot reroll seller contents.

Before inspection, the native dealership shows aggregate condition, runs/drives, appearance and labelled estimated HP/torque/weight/rating/dyno. Estimates use factory hardware and public aggregate condition, never actual hidden damage, history or fluid quantities. Full Stats, individual parts/settings, exact supplies and setup/wheelspin/drivetrain warnings are withheld. Basic comparison and F8/legacy follow the same knowledge state.

Inspect opens the existing confirmation dialog with the configured fee, balance and resulting balance. The initial Operating Policy version 3 fee is $50. Explicit confirmation commits one receipt and listing-specific knowledge; Cancel, insufficient funds, stale/duplicate requests or failed saves cannot disclose or charge. Inspection reveals actual curves, all collapsible stats, fitted faults/history/liquids and warnings. It cannot repair/refill, change price, select a car or alter owned assets. Knowledge survives reload and does not reveal other listings.

Buying with or without inspection transfers actual part identities, individual damage, tune, history and fluid quantities. The owned page retains full condition, ordinary maintenance and a compact History info action. Fixed first-car choices stay known/safe with fixed supplies and a maintenance reserve; this still does not guarantee a first career win. Previously disclosed saved offers migrate known without retroactive damage, healing or regeneration.

The new disclosure revision invalidates only changed dealership estimates. A captured knowledge flag prevents old estimated figures surviving inspection. Stats visibility is keyed by listing identity. Visual review also exposed an existing isolated-preview migration that fabricated removed interior trim: previews now explicitly use current equipment/driveline metadata, retaining optional omissions and exactly matching the purchased car's weight/output.
