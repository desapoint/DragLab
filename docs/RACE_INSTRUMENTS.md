# Race instruments — approved direction (2026-10-14)

## Dashboard and pause refinement (2026-10-14)

- Replace the blank lower strip with a low, textured car dashboard. Use stable noise/grain, a shallow raised instrument-cluster hood on the left, and a restrained cropped steering-wheel hint. The gauges are expected to overflow above the dash; do not increase its height just to contain them.
- Move race information into this lower zone. Reuse the native palette and sharp procedural geometry; do not turn the dash into a plain rectangle or a new per-screen control theme.
- Keep the original large round manual shift knob, with no exposed H-gate plate; animate its position through the H path. The wheel hint needs a wider radius and thicker rim while keeping its upper edge. Round the shaft ends and move its boot exit around a hidden lower pivot. Bring 1/3/5 closer to the boot while preserving 2/4/6. Animation must follow the car’s actual running shift duration/remaining time, including faster hardware, and stop advancing while paused.
- Render installed selector hardware: animate an H-pattern manual through the neutral crossgate, use a fore/aft selector for sequential manual, a framed numeric indicator for DCT, an automatic indicator for automatic, and N/D for CVT. Driving-assist mode must not change physical selector type. Drive animation from the actual engaged gear and shift handover, without changing shift timing or simulating an extra gearbox.
- Hide the legacy pedal/shift buttons and key-hint row in the new driving interface and disable their invisible pointer targets. Keep legacy comparison available. Bindings belong in the pause settings/controls dialog.
- Pause must reuse the shared modal surfaces, corner accents, close hover, buttons, scrollbars and sliders. Offer Resume, quick Sound, view Controls and Quit race; quitting needs explicit confirmation and must retain used resources/wear through the existing durable exit.
- Race settings are contained in that dialog and deliberately limited. Do not open the full normal-menu Settings page, its tabs/save workflow, progression/reset or simulation/difficulty tuning. The initial limited editable choices are master/opponent audio only; controls are read-only. Audio changes remain local drafts until the explicit action in the dialog. Back/X/Escape discard unapplied audio, keep the race paused on child pages, and return to pause; only Resume/main-close resumes.

The initial instrument checkpoint implemented speed/RPM/shifter. The dashboard/pause refinement above supersedes its layout and control-strip decisions. Extra gauge families and variants still require their own reviewed increment.

- Start with analog road-speed and RPM gauges together on the left and a shifter-shaped engaged-gear display on the right. “Speed” on the shifter means the selected gear; road speed stays on its own gauge. Show N in neutral and D for a running CVT instead of invented stepped gears.
- Circular faces do not require a full-circle needle sweep. Support partial-circle and semicircular layouts through presentation configuration, independently of the simulation. The first layout uses a 270° sweep.
- Make instrument faces, scale geometry, palette, labels and telemetry separable so future themes can reuse the same readings. Future alternatives include analog and digital variants, including the requested eight-segment treatment. Do not add a variant-selection menu yet.
- The RPM gauge has a redline band, derived from the minimum safe installed-engine/hardware RPM rating and the engine redline. This is distinct from the electronic cut target or the recommended shift point. Do not draw a recommended shift tick/target on the RPM dial.
- Retain the plan for visible shift lights, single or multiple according to the actual purchased/installed hardware. A multi-light system must require suitable hardware; a display theme cannot grant it. Honor the actual supported threshold/tuning and transmission behavior. Shift-light migration is deferred from this first instrument checkpoint; the original interface remains available with F8.
- Fuel and heat gauges are part of the intended instrument family. Heat instrumentation is optional: some cars lack suitable factory gauges. Later aftermarket hardware can grant temperature/heat detail (including better oil information) and oil-pressure instrumentation. Do not invent sensors or grant that information through a theme. These readings are not wired in this checkpoint.
- Reuse the shared grey/red palette, font and sharp vector rendering. Keep essential text concise. The track stays visible; no main-menu navbar over driving. Anchor instruments around the existing controls at compact/windowed, 1080p and scaled 4K sizes.
- Presentation reads the authoritative live race, with no duplicated physics, telemetry worker, profile recalculation or save on display updates. Preserve held throttle across keyboard/mouse shifts, neutral staging, F8 comparison and existing warm-up/pause/results controls while those await their own checkpoints.

Technical completion and user visual acceptance remain separate. Do not advance to boost/pedals/optional telemetry/shift lights or later race screens without another request.
