## Rules for every subphase

- Preserve a runnable race and the existing engine/sound bench. Introduce new isolated domain fixtures first, then connect the same model to the real player; never claim an override fixture validates road dynamics.
- Use unique IDs and recoverable state changes from the start. Old saves/records remain backed up; migrations must be versioned, repeatable and non-destructive on failure. Never create omitted OEM parts on every load or heal parts during migration/retry.
- Keep experimental bench state separate from player fuel, inventory, condition and cash. Expand live telemetry/replay as fields arrive rather than defer tooling until all mechanics are finished.
- Inspect held W/Space, E launch/upshift, manual clutch, keyboard/slider ownership, pause and refuel/swap controls at relevant checkpoints. No new button may inadvertently cancel held gas. Hardware swaps are allowed only outside an active run; bench live refuelling follows its declared behavior.
- Keep provisional part prices functional in Slice 2. Player refuelling is free throughout Slice 2; it becomes paid in 3D. Model-specific wear/fuel behavior is calibrated and tested separately from economic prices/rewards.
- For each checkpoint, record affected source/data, fixture configuration/model versions, meaningful checks, any changed benchmark results and remaining limits. The current 523-check build is a baseline, not a required future check count. Changes in physical outcomes need explained new evidence, not weakened safety or ownership assertions.
- Run representative small fixtures while developing, broaden to all supported stock cars/hardware at the assigned gate, and keep an exportable passing checkpoint. Do not enable unfinished destructive ownership operations or quietly ship two contradictory authoritative physics models.
