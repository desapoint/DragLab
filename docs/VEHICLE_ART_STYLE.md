# Approved vehicle sprite style - 2026-10-18

The user approved `art/concepts/juniper-raster-v1/04-strong-outline-preview.png` and authorized the remaining roster in this style. This supersedes the rejected procedural/vector body trials and the earlier soft/glossy previews. Use raster sprites with believable real-car proportions, strong dark silhouette/panel outlines, flat cel-shaded colour blocks and restrained graphic reflections. Preserve individual old/modern model identity rather than stretching four shared bodies; body length, roof/cabin proportions, overhangs and axle spacing may differ. Fictional design years are art direction, not manufacturer facts or new physics specifications.

Generate one coherent exterior master per model family. Trims may share their model's body. Face right in strict orthographic side view, with transparent safety margins. No baked wheels, tires, brakes, ground or shadows in the exterior. Wheel arches remain transparent and retain the natural fender edges. Tires/rims, rotating rotors, stationary calipers and local arch backs remain separate layers. Distinct shop wheel artwork is a separate component task, not permission to draw wheels into car bodies.

Paint customization affects painted body panels only. Extract nonpaint fixtures from the master into protected layers: glass, lights/lenses/indicators, rubber/black trim, grille, unpainted mirrors/handles, chrome and similar fixtures retain their original colours. Painted roof/pillars, hood, doors, fenders, bumpers and rockers remain colourable; avoid losing dark painted shading or contaminating glass/lights. Keep strong linework and cel shading. Verify masks and several contrasting repaints at native and game scale.

Keep master bytes, exact prompts, source hashes, measured axle/arch geometry, layer manifests and replayable preparation scripts. Review transparency, body/fixture isolation, paint coverage and assembled occlusion/rotation. Use existing stable car IDs and saved paint; artwork must not modify simulation dimensions, mechanical assets, condition, tuning, resources or saves. Retain previous production artwork for recovery. Record technical completion separately from the style approval.
