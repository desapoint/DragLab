# S06d2a exact live display prices

This narrow optimization keeps `Valuations.LoosePriceView` and its `Func<string,bool,int>` contract unchanged. Each fresh wrapper still validates the policy and the complete owned profile/catalogue once. Every read still checks the captured profile revision, and resolves the current buyer, catalogue, and owned part or unit from their live lists. It keeps the existing `List.Find` ordering and current-context behavior; there is no price cache or persistent index.

Quote validation retains the original strict `MechanicsOwnership.Validate` first pass and its first-error result. If that error is dealer-specific, the quote-only second pass reruns the same full validator with one narrow allowance: a dealer row's serialized `isUnit` flag may disagree with its sole populated asset identity. SharedInventory still rejects missing identities, both populated assets, invalid/duplicate parts and descendants, invalid units, invalid prices, and all other dealer errors in their existing order. The pass reads those identities directly and does not normalize or mutate the profile, clone it, or encode JSON. The regular validator remains strict.

Display amounts now call the same part and individual-element scalar arithmetic used by the receipt-producing quote path. Receipt line allocation and dynamic explanation strings are skipped when the shared calculation receives no receipt list. The complete `Capture`/`Calculate` path remains available for payment, receipt generation, and a development-only exact-value oracle. Quote rounding remains checked `Math.Round(..., MidpointRounding.AwayFromZero)` after the same finite/range check.

`NativePartDisplayPriceChecks.Run(Action<bool,string>, string)` covers normal, worn, failed, history-bearing, individual tire-corner, loose-unit, quality/policy, same-revision mutation, revision rejection and missing-ID cases against the retained no-snapshot legacy calculation. Dealer-specific checks cover stale part and unit discriminators through a fresh `LoosePriceView` factory and price call, empty inactive wrappers, conflicting/missing payloads, duplicate dealer IDs and duplicate descendants. They verify unchanged source state and zero attributed encoder calls on the stale-flag recovery calls. It also records fresh wrapper plus per-record price cost and current-thread allocations for 24, 100 and 1,000 part records and individual units. `PRICE-COST` diagnostics separately time vehicle listings, paid economy, garage business, full ownership validation and quote validation on the seeded first-starter fixture, then repeat quote validation with 36 additional inventory parts. The check writes `S06D2A_LIVE_PRICES.txt` under the supplied fixture directory. These measurements are descriptive; this code checkpoint alone does not claim a frame-gate pass.
