# S05b direct refill writer decisions

Baseline `ff9102e`: reviewed S05a storage migration is locally committed (66 focused +72 isolated storage checks). Implement the approved dormant direct writer next. The S06 ordered owner queue and gameplay switch remain separate; final no-runtime-JSON/no-full-profile-save-clone gates remain mandatory.

## Owner binding and read authority

`TypedSaveSlotBinding` is an immutable capability captured by the state owner from the actual selected absolute database path, save slot and reset generation. It is not inferred from a filename, derived from the request's claimed slot, or installed as a new database authority. A command captures its expected binding; the writer independently receives the owner's actual binding and compares path/slot/generation before database access. The worker uses only that bound path. S06 must construct/revalidate these bindings automatically from actual owner selection and serialize slot/reset changes; arbitrary caller-supplied labels cannot substitute for that provider. Dormant verification may construct explicit fixture bindings.

S04's detached read set already captures car/engine IDs, availability, active-run state, resolved capacities/prices and full fluid/economy observations. The unconverted car payload does not have typed columns for every dependency. Their source is the valid owner graph at the exact global storage/ledger fence, checked again by S06 before admission. Strict transactional commit CAS excludes intervening supported writes. The direct writer additionally checks scoped SQL car/engine owner identities/definitions, typed economy/fluid values and affected record hashes. It must not decode legacy car JSON, fabricate row versions or scan unrelated state to recreate owner facts. This is a trusted-owner contract, not authentication against a caller forging a capability or rewriting disk rows and hashes.

The direct writer cannot merge/retire unconverted race journal state. A new operation with pending checkpoint work is rejected through a bounded existence query until the owner has established safe ordering. An already committed receipt may still reconcile under its original identity. The S06 admission step must resolve this ordering without adding a full-profile persistence fallback to the pilot.

The owner prepares storage resources before dispatch, as existing load/save already does. The direct command opens only an already-current database and never triggers migration, all-row validation or Unity resource loading on its worker. An unprepared store or old-schema input is rejected for normal owner loading first.

## Command and durable outcome

Freeze one accepted confirmation tick before preparing the successful S04 plan; it must equal the plan's capture/planning and business receipt ticks. Physics identity is supplied explicitly by the owner. A separate current execution time validates a new command's quote; retry never substitutes that time into intent. Stable intent encoding/order/bounds and receipt identity follow S05P_DECISIONS exactly, with full canonical-byte comparison in addition to SHA.

`TypedFluidRefillIntent` contains only binding, original immutable request/fences, physics, accepted tick and canonical bytes. Reconciliation accepts that value directly, without a successful planner result, current car, prices, fluids or old read set. A fresh `TypedFluidRefillCommand` composes the intent and its validated successful S04 plan for writing. Restart verification must reconstruct a new intent from retained request facts after history eviction and car removal, rather than retaining the original in-memory plan. A current read-set clock/availability change may reject a new operation but cannot prevent constructing its original stable intent for recovery.

The existing schema stores only affected fluid quantities in the durable result: cost, cash after, oil/coolant additions and resulting quantities, car ID and domain receipt ID; the operation receipt supplies intent, fences and clock. It does not store the twelve untouched fluid fields or the bounded business receipt's ordering number. Do not fabricate these from later current state on replay. A fresh outcome may include the just-inserted business receipt as a publication patch; a replay may include that history row while retained, and must still reconcile when it has legitimately been evicted. S06 applies only a fresh fenced patch and must not overwrite advanced owner cash/resources with historical replay values. Full fourteen-field before/after preservation remains required for a new commit and independent cold-load checks.

## Bounded history and integrity

Append a monotonic business `receipt_order` (`MAX+1`) and evict only the oldest receipt/items at the256 limit. Do not renumber255 unchanged history rows for one refill. Full typed reads accept a contiguous nonnegative order window starting above zero, restoring its original list order. Transitional legacy captures may still emit0-based orders while their family remains unconverted; verify both directions of interoperability. Generic operation/result rows survive business-history eviction and car removal for their reset generation.

Under the existing single writer gate and SQLite transaction, verify only declared old records/hashes, XOR out their old hashes and XOR in explicit after-image hashes. Update only affected typed rows, history eviction, operation/result records, integrity entries and metadata. No `Rows`, `ReadAllTypedRecords`, `RebuildIntegrity`, relationship `Sync`, JSON projection, whole-profile validation or copy. Publish cache commit/token changes only after COMMIT. Failure/reconciliation tests must distinguish definite rollback from uncertain acknowledgement and must prove no duplicate effect after restart.
