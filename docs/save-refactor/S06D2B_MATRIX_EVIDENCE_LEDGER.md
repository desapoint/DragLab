- **Legacy normalization findings** describe observed `Ensure`/serialization behavior. They do not approve those effects for preview. No typed no-heal implementation is present.
- The reports cover different reviewed source freezes and overlapping subsets. Do not add their assertion counts or treat one trace as a rerun of another source revision.
