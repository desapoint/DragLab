# Fuel simulation — 2I foundation / 3D pricing

The shared CarSimulation burns fuel only on simulation ticks (120 Hz). Idle flow scales with displacement; output demand uses fictional brake-specific consumption, with carburetor mixture allowance, neutral rev demand, injection overrun cut, fuel-versus-ignition cut and anti-lag enrichment. FuelSpec exposes tank capacity, density, location, idle calibration at 2 litres and BSFC in the vehicle Inspector. Older serialized zero idle/BSFC fields use the documented 0.8 L/h and 0.30 kg/kWh defaults. This is a provisional game model, not a manufacturer fuel-economy certification or a complete carburetor/fuel-pressure model.

Actual quantity changes tank mass, total translating mass, CG and axle loads. Exhausted fuel stops combustion and permits wheel/engine momentum to decay without declaring damaged internals. Refuel then explicitly restart (Enter in the physical bench). Fuel loss is quantized to a physical tick; it is not offline/business time. Paused simulation consumes none.

Garage: typed quantity and slider limited by tank free space, paid selected refuel and fill in 3D; only accepted litres are charged. Physical bench: Diagnostics → Mass → Fuel controls, with the same capped actions. Live refuel preserves motion, pedal ownership, recording choice and component condition; it starts a new independently replayable recorded segment. The bench tank remains separate from player fuel. Quantity, cumulative usage and flow are exported in complete chronological CSV and captured checkpoints. Garage class benchmark is explicitly a full-tank reference; current inspection and racing use owned quantity.

Owned quantity/usage persist with wear on race return, retry, pause and normal quit, and survive reload without automatic refilling. Hard-crash recovery remains the 2K recoverable-run checkpoint gate. Refuelling was free in Slice 2. Current 3D adds paid fuel and basic recovery; see [paid operations](PAID_OPERATIONS_AND_RECOVERY.md). Final balance and the wider career/idle loop remain 3E–3H.
