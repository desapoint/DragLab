# Mass and load model — phase 2F

The shared `CarSimulation` now separates the dry installed build, driver, payload and current fuel input from wheel and flywheel rotational inertia. The declared vehicle reference mass is OEM curb mass with a full tank and no driver or payload. Factory wheel/flywheel values are already in that reference; replacing them adds their difference exactly once. Other installed parts add/subtract their physical replacement mass relative to the OEM recipe. Hardware measurements, placement and structural share are fictional calibration, not manufacturer data.

Structural body upgrades reduce the modeled structure only (35% of reference dry mass), rather than shrinking the engine, fuel, driver and every other installed part. Interior and comfort equipment are independently removable and remain reusable inventory. The factory recipe and stable owned part identities are unchanged.

Total translating mass = dry installed build + driver + payload + fuel quantity × density. Longitudinal and vertical mass moments include those inputs and part replacement/removal deltas. Static front/rear normal loads sum to total weight. Acceleration/braking load transfer uses the calculated CG height and axle distribution. The old equivalent wheel inertia contribution remains a temporary acceleration approximation until 2G–2H integrate independent wheel contact; it is never added to gravitational load.

Garage tuning exposes driver weight, payload weight, longitudinal payload position and payload height through the existing draft/Apply/Revert workflow. Stats show the mass breakdown, CG and axle loads; changes appear in the pinned summary. Physical bench diagnostics expose those inputs and fuel quantity independently of keyboard pedal ownership. Edits retain motion, RPM, pause and recording state. Active recordings split at the change and capture a physical checkpoint; the preceding timeline remains replayable with its original inputs/configuration.

Fuel quantity at this phase is a mass input. Consumption, empty-tank behavior and the capped free-refuel operation belong to 2I; prices remain Slice 3. Owned tanks initialize once, and initialized empty tanks are not refilled by subsequent migration. A versioned one-time driver migration supplies 75 kg to pre-mass saves and presets; later values are retained. Validation checks state without migrating or repairing it.

Physical exports capture the actual starting quantity and include total/dry/driver/payload/fuel mass, CG height/position and dynamic axle loads in every sample. Model `draglab-mass-load-v7` requires re-recording older model captures rather than pretending they have unchanged physics. Deep checkpoint copies retain fuel and all existing mechanical histories and pedal/shift/induction state.

Dedicated gate evidence is in `reports/phases/2f/verification.txt`; Windows controls and the saved checkpoint are recorded separately. The career verification now uses the actual owned installed-component resolver, meaningful free tuning and retained cabin removal, with earned purchases and no injected money. It preserves the requirement that the starter can complete all four authored career events. This is a progression reachability check with fresh copied conditions per probe; later economic and interrupted-run endurance gates remain their planned scope.

Subsequent checkpoints: 2H replaced the equivalent-mass acceleration approximation with independent wheel/contact inertia; 2I added live fuel consumption/refuelling; 2J separated rim and rubber mass so lightweight wheels cannot remove tire heat/life reserve. See the integrated audit and the respective phase documents. The earlier statements above describe the archived 2F increment.
