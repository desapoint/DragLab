# Garage jobs and offline income (3F)

Open **Business**, or **Garage > Jobs / expansion / claim income**. Recovery also links here when no car is owned. Income starts at $100/hour with an eight-hour cap. Expansion costs $1,200 / $2,400 / $3,600 and gives levels 2–4: $180 / $300 / $500 per hour, 12 / 18 / 24 hour caps, and 2 / 3 / 4 simultaneous job bays. Existing car storage remains unrestricted; expansion does not invalidate an existing collection.

The saved accrual contract contains its hourly rate, cap, epoch and fractional cash. Claims retain fractions. Expansion settles the old contract before applying the new level. Editing authoring rates cannot retrospectively increase elapsed earnings: a claim settles the captured rate before adopting the current policy. Legacy higher-level saves retain the original eight-hour cap for their first claim, then adopt the expanded cap. A backwards clock cannot move the epoch or release a job early; reload preserves that wait.

Select an actual owned car, then assign an eligible display job. The selected instance, attached engine and installed packs are reserved until **Claim** or **Cancel**. These prototype jobs keep engines off and consume no driving fuel, fluids or wear. They use installed HP/drivetrain, reputation and garage-level requirements. Captured deadlines and rewards persist offline; later policy edits do not alter an assigned contract. Completed unclaimed jobs remain reserved. Cancellation releases immediately without payment.

Reserved builds cannot race, enter the physical bench, Apply tuning, receive service, remove/swap components, detach engines or be disposed of. Their car, engine and chassis quotes remain inspectable but cannot authorize a sale. Another owned instance, including a second copy of the same model, can race or take another job. Blueprint capture and inspection do not transfer ownership. Claim/cancel and reservation release commit together; a failed write leaves the unpaid job and all reserved IDs intact.

Edit `Game/Assets/DragLab/Resources/Data/Economy/GarageBusinessPolicy.asset` in its inspector. Rates, caps, expansion costs, bays and offers are authorable. `DragLab > Prepare Garage Business Policy` only creates missing data; **Verify Garage Jobs and Idle** exports a car/offer eligibility matrix and clock/transaction/reservation checks. Job definitions and save state have independent version guards. A stored job verifies its exact resource/tune/component snapshot rather than healing or reconstructing it.

Recovery work remains a separate bounded income route requiring no car, useful when all builds are broken or salvaged. Earnings fund the same paid operating/component transactions as race rewards. Prices and durations are provisional until the 3G scenario pass; 3H remains the integrated release audit.
