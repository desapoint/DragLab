# Slice 3 acceptance contract

Slice 3 is 3A–3H. Its developer acceptance gate verifies a playable paid operating/progression loop using the existing authoritative simulation. Final art, production UI/audio, storefront distribution, human economy pacing and Android/controller device acceptance are separate work. The dated [full audit](../reports/SLICE_3_AUDIT.md) records actual results and open limits; this contract alone does not declare completion.

| Scope | Required behavior | Primary verification |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 3B assemblies/configurations | Stored/partial engines retain actual children; compatible transfers; virtual named engine/car/tune setups; explicit preview/Apply; reuse existing parts and buy missing parts without compulsory preceding ranks | Assembly domain and actual Windows setups |

`tools/Test-SliceThreeIntegration.ps1 -ShowPlayer` runs the full domain/native suite and records a per-gate manifest. `-DomainOnly` and `-NativeOnly -SkipBuild` allow disjoint runs against an already accepted build. Use a fresh RunName; fixture outputs are immutable. Native tests show only their own development player, and the interruption tool terminates only its verified fixture process after a durable checkpoint. Test cash, initial faults and accelerated deadlines are explicitly isolated and do not manipulate the user's save or production clock.

The runner restores historical tracked evidence byte-for-byte after domain reruns and stores the new evidence under the 3H validation folder. Final acceptance reports select the relevant reviewable text/matrices; raw profiles, screenshots, audio/log captures and complete source/Windows archives remain local. Passing totals from overlapping suites are reported individually rather than added into a misleading unique-case count.

Reconciliation retained from approved changes: modular direct upgrade/downgrade, actual old-part inventory/individual faults, 25% reduced fuel/wear, hardware-owned tuning permissions and installed protections, independent wheel/contact physics and AWD failure paths, fluids and limited repair, real-clock rebuild reservations, unit preferences, and bench isolation. None is silently simplified for affordability. Garage display jobs are engine-off exhibits; driving customer jobs and rotating used-car markets remain later content. Competition tier letters label events and do not assert each rival has the same benchmark performance class.

Approved extension during final audit: heat management ON exposes ambient/optimal/live race-entry temperatures; OFF holds optimum and disables entry choices. Per-car thermal clocks survive saved/interrupted runs, and optional pre-lights warm-up retains physical heat, tire wear and consumed resources. The runner includes a focused thermal domain gate, actual Windows settings/warm-up and all eight stock races. See [thermal contract](HEAT_AND_RACE_TURNAROUND.md).
