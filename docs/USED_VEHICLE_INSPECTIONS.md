# Used vehicle inspection and risk

Authorized 2026-10-11 after the variety/rival checkpoint. This adds one dealership workflow; it does not advance another Slice 4 page.

Saved seller contents are authoritative. Normal used listings can have neglected service, overheating/compression damage, coolant leaks or unrepaired engine/brake/clutch/shaft/pump failures. Their fuel, oil and coolant quantities vary independently. Generation is seeded and retained; browsing/reloading/inspection cannot reroll or repair a car. Short run/drive observations use an isolated copy of the ordinary simulation. Bad brakes do not stop combustion; drivetrain and engine faults keep their actual consequences.

Before inspection, show overall condition and observed engine-running/driving status, public model information and clearly labelled estimated HP/torque/weight/rating/dyno. Estimates come from the factory model, aggregate condition and a fixed listing estimate seed, without actual hidden part faults/history/resources. Keep visible wheel/body appearance accurate. Hide exact fitted parts, individual wear/faults, detailed saved settings, actual fluid quantities, history and wheelspin/drivetrain/setup warnings. Comparisons use the same public estimate and cannot unlock detailed stats. F8/legacy commerce follows the same knowledge state.

A $50 fee is the initial authored operating policy (version 3). The native Inspect action opens the shared confirmation dialog with fee/balance; Cancel leaves state/disk untouched. Confirmation uses immutable paid quotes, whole-profile rollback and a receipt; it reveals only that exact listing. Failed persistence, duplicate/stale confirmations, sold/rotated listings and insufficient funds cannot charge or disclose. Inspection neither repairs nor refills, and does not alter asking price or selected ownership. Known/new/first-starter offers cannot be charged. Persist knowledge across reloads; new listings need their own inspection.

Inspection reveals real current/no-wear curves, all detailed stats, exact fluids, faults/history and setup warnings. The Stats panel opens using the existing collapsible groups and comparison controls. Buying transfers actual component identities, individual damage, histories and liquids even without inspection; ownership reveals full condition and retains a History info action. Existing maintenance/replacement remains authoritative.

The fixed three-choice first-car set stays fully disclosed, mechanically safe and affordable, with fixed fuel/oil/coolant supplies and a reserve. This does not guarantee a first career win or immunity to abuse. Previously visible saved listings migrate as already disclosed; no retroactive hidden defects, part replacement, resource refill or stock reroll is added. Metadata extensions are validated separately from physical contents.

Developer tools: edit the inspection fee in the Operating Policy asset; `UsedInspectionVerify` prepares the versioned default only once and checks public/truth separation, seeded stock, payment/rollback, exact purchase and saved knowledge. `Test-NativeMenu.ps1 -InspectionOnly` exercises actual controls, comparison/F8, cancellation, payment, disclosure and ownership in isolated saves.
