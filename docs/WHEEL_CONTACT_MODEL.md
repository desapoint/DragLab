# Wheel contact model — phase 2G

The domain solver `WheelContactDynamics` tracks four independent angular speeds/angles, signed slip and longitudinal contact forces. Translational acceleration uses actual carried mass; every wheel has its own rotational inertia. Contact impulses are bounded by surface friction and normal load, with modeled load sensitivity and a sliding-grip curve. Slip is measured from wheel peripheral speed versus body speed; it is never replaced by a demand estimate.

Open axles retain left/right angular freedom. Limited-slip preload and torque bias transfer torque against relative speed and dissipate internal energy. Welded/spool axles constrain their wheel speeds. A locked center constrains front/rear axle **means**, retaining left/right freedom of open axles; it does not weld all four wheels. Impulse projection respects those constraints during contact and braking. Brake work exists only when actual brake torque slows rotation. Contact slip-work is stored separately per wheel; differential loss is tracked separately and included in total dissipated work.

Drive work, rotating/translating kinetic energy, brake work, contact/differential loss and external resistance work close their accounting checks. The forward-only body model stops at zero speed; reverse gear and vehicle pitch/yaw are outside this drag slice. Contact calibration is fictional data, not a validated tire-manufacturer model. No thermal or tire wear behavior is asserted at this phase.

Use **DragLab → Vehicle Workbench → Wheel contact lab** for a directly applied torque fixture. It has editable carried mass, radius, wheel inertia, front/rear axle torque, brake torque, split surface grip, axle differential, center lock and differential health/disconnection. It supports Run/Pause, one tick, reset, cutting drive torque, a repeatable launch/open-drive/brake scenario, live wheel RPM/slip/force/energy and optional CSV recording off by default. CSV rows include the physical configuration and applied inputs. This fixture has no engine/transmission: phase 2H connects this same solver to the shared race/AI/physical bench. The existing game remains on the 2F model until that connection passes its gate.
