Coverage and limits

The Effects section contains 124 examples from the actual 120 Hz CarSimulation, published through the game's EngineSoundController at 60 Hz. Before and after receive the same immutable captured frames. Hardware/controls fixtures enable the requested test settings without modifying player cars. Fuel/ignition cuts follow the engine's permissions: carburetors use ignition cuts and diesels use fuel cuts. Anti-lag/preload examples use petrol cars with factory turbos. Row counters show real generated pops, limiting and release events. Cold idle has no invented starter-motor sound; shift handovers retain the actual transmission behavior.

All 13 authored car/trim definitions, current 3/4/6/8-cylinder families, petrol and diesel, factory fueling and available carburetor-to-EFI conversions. Engine combinations cross every engine/fueling variant with all six induction topologies and factory exhaust plus four presets. Focused groups cover compact/street/large turbo sizes, recirculated/hiss/chirp/flutter release, pulley 0.85/0.90/1.00/1.20 and boost calibration 0.80/1.00/1.20. Continuous controls use these representative values; focused groups keep the other settings fixed.

After: deeper crossplane V8 bank voice, softer healthy petrol mechanical ticking, small seeded amplitude variation and restrained compressor texture. Diesel retains its authored mechanical clatter. Other engines retain their own core voice; the V8 growl is not copied onto every cylinder layout. Pop and release-valve generators are retained. TBI and port injection can sound identical under controlled healthy conditions; no artificial distinction is added.

Engine-matrix entries use scripted RPM/throttle/load and audition boost/spool trajectories; Effects entries use captured real simulation frames. Neither section validates every hypothetical build. No oil-pressure, wear, knock or damage sound redesign is included. Factory profiles retain stock parameters; the before generator is the unchanged pre-integration version. Octane/cetane have no direct audio parameter. No extra ethanol, direct-injection petrol, Roots or centrifugal hardware is claimed. Backfire examples use the existing exhaust-pop model.

Laboratory examples cover the generator’s accepted counts 1–16 and extra layouts. They are not new buyable engines; odd/high counts inherit current synthesis limitations and have not been approved as realistic voices. They are hidden initially.

Each pair shares one achievable loudness target toward −20 LUFS with a −3 dBTP ceiling. Strong transients can lower that target for both sides. Files are lossless mono 48 kHz FLAC. Identical pairs share one file; all labelled cases remain in the matrix. No tire, opponent, camera-distance or game-mixer audio is mixed in.
