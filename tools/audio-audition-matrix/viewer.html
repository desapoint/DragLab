Coverage and limits

All 13 authored car/trim definitions, current 3/4/6/8-cylinder families, petrol and diesel, factory fueling and available carburetor-to-EFI conversions. Engine combinations cross every engine/fueling variant with all six induction topologies and factory exhaust plus four presets. Focused groups cover compact/street/large turbo sizes, recirculated/hiss/chirp/flutter release, pulley 0.85/0.90/1.00/1.20 and boost calibration 0.80/1.00/1.20. Continuous controls use these representative values; focused groups keep the other settings fixed.

After: deeper crossplane V8 bank voice, softer healthy petrol mechanical ticking, small seeded amplitude variation and restrained compressor texture. Diesel retains its authored mechanical clatter. Other engines retain their own core voice; the V8 growl is not copied onto every cylinder layout. Release-valve character is unchanged by this draft. TBI and port injection can sound identical under these controlled healthy conditions; no artificial distinction is added.

These are scripted sound auditions, not races or validated compatible builds. RPM/throttle/load are controlled inputs; boost/spool are audition trajectories using the authored hardware scales. No oil-pressure, wear, knock or damage sound redesign is included. Factory profiles use captured stock parameters through a standalone adapter; the before generator itself is copied unchanged. Octane/cetane have no direct audio parameter; changes caused by knock/cold behavior need simulation-driven tests. No extra ethanol, direct-injection petrol, Roots or centrifugal hardware is claimed.

Laboratory examples cover the generator’s accepted counts 1–16 and extra layouts. They are not new buyable engines; odd/high counts inherit current synthesis limitations and have not been approved as realistic voices. They are hidden initially.

Each side is constant-gain loudness matched toward −20 LUFS with a −3 dBTP ceiling. Files are lossless mono 48 kHz FLAC. Identical before/after pairs share one file; all labelled cases remain in the matrix. No tire, opponent, camera-distance or game-mixer audio is mixed in.
