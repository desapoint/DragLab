Fidelity and Unity integration notes

Source-matched architecture and many constants: PressureCombustionCore (four-stroke pressure pulse tables and runner delay), EngineSoundSynth, ExhaustFilter, TurboAcoustics, IntakeRushSynth, ReleaseAudioSynth, AfterfireLabSource, AfterfirePipeResponse, TireSoundSynth, TracksidePropagation and the Tempest definition. The pulse-envelope grids, many envelope coefficients, exhaust-stage part presets, gain factors and afterfire V3 settings are transcribed from those sources.

Explicit acoustic controls: runner length, collector echo path and manifold resonator length are geometric surrogates; pressure attack, blowdown and rarefaction are timing controls in milliseconds. They approximate the original acoustic response with preset-expanded physical values, not a full waveguide or CFD simulation. Changing layout or cylinders does not automatically overwrite those settings.

Browser approximations: the test race uses a lightweight RPM, boost and traction model rather than the game's Race/CarState physical solver. Afterfire onset is controlled by manual events and seeded probabilistic triggers instead of the actual ExhaustMixtureModel 8-zone fuel/oxygen/heat transport and funding; its V3 source includes oversampling but is not guaranteed sample-identical to C#. Multi-path air filtering and tire contact inputs are likewise simplified. The dedicated wheel-spin controls inject deterministic slip and wheel surface speed into the tire voices without simulating spinning wheels or vehicle physics; only the chosen axle receives that test input. AudioWorklet audio output and off-line WAVs use the same browser renderer, but neither is guaranteed PCM-identical to Unity.

Vehicle presets now only fill editable runner/collector/cavity geometry and combustion-envelope times; there is no acoustic archetype switch in the browser DSP. To apply accepted tuning in-game: Export Unity spec translates these geometry values to the current C# legacy AcousticPreset.Automatic plus scales (approximation only); Export game mapping retains the explicit physical dimensions for a future native implementation; Export full preset keeps the complete test state. An actual Unity migration is required to remove legacy acoustic profiles from the game. Processing bypasses are diagnostic and do not correspond to persisted gameplay settings without additional C# hooks. The initial listening level is reduced; avoid high headphone volume during full-pass bypasses.
